Cover Sheet Merger

Prepend a cover sheet to any Word document. Files never leave your computer — everything runs in your browser.

How it works & known limits

The tool inserts a Next Page section break between the cover sheet and your document, so each section keeps its own margins, headers, footers, page numbering, and orientation. This is the same approach Word itself recommends.

The main document stays as the base (keeps all its styles, theme, headers/footers). The cover sheet's content is prepended; its embedded images are copied in and renamed if needed.

What may not transfer perfectly:
  • Custom styles defined only in the cover sheet — they'll fall back to the main document's style of the same name (Word's built-in styles like Title, Heading 1 will look right).
  • The cover sheet's own headers/footers — by design, they don't carry over (the section break gives the cover its own blank header area).
  • Complex features: tracked changes, comments, footnotes that exist in the cover sheet, and macros may be dropped.

For most cover sheets (title block, logo, project info, signature lines) the result is indistinguishable from a manual Word merge.
